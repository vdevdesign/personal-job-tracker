Google Sheets Live Sync

Two-Way Sync via Google Apps Script

Paste your deployed Apps Script Web App URL (ending in /exec) so edits on your phone automatically update your Google Sheet, and updates made in Gemini sync right back to your phone.

Need the Apps Script code?

In your Google Sheet: Extensions → Apps Script → paste code → Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me, Access: Anyone).
